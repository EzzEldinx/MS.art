MAHMOUD SAÏD

Art • Heritage • Exhibition Design

Where heritage becomes space, and history meets contemporary practice.

At the intersection of art history, modernism, and contemporary practice, where heritage becomes spatial narrative.

Mahmoud Saïd is an Alexandria-based architect and artist whose practice operates at the intersection of architectural history, cultural heritage, and exhibition design. Working at the Centre d’Études Alexandrines (CNRS), he engages in the research and documentation of the city’s built heritage while developing research-based exhibitions that translate archival material into spatial and visual narratives.

His work bridges architecture and contemporary artistic practice, with a particular focus on portrait painting in dialogue with art historical references and contemporary expression, and has been presented across exhibitions, museums, art galleries, and cultural institutions in Alexandria and Cairo.
